RockGuard: Rockfall Early Warning System

Agentic AI Hackathon 2025 · Ulster University, UK

Monitors open-pit mines across India and predicts rockfall risk for each one. An ML ensemble (Random Forest, XGBoost, Naive Bayes) is blended with a Fuzzy AHP score into one hybrid risk level, from Low to Critical.

  • Live monitor: an India map of 8 mines colored by risk, with live alerts whenever a mine's risk rises or eases and the factor driving it.
  • Risk assessment: each mine's score broken down into geological, environmental, seismic, and human-impact factors.
  • Action plan: recommendations such as evacuation, slope drainage, or a blasting review, plus a generated PDF report.
  • Trends: monthly rainfall against the long-term normal, and a live risk-score chart.

Team CoreSight, 6 members. I built the ML models behind the risk score.
